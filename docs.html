Kipuu Docs

Guides for everyone on your team.

Getting started

🚀

Create your company account

Sign up in under a minute. Your company gets its own private space, and you become its owner.

Files and folders

📁

Folders, uploads and downloads

Organize files in folders, upload by dragging them in, and download them with one click.

🔗

Share files with people outside your company

Send a link to a file or folder. It works without an account, expires when you choose, and you can turn it off.

▶️

Watch, listen and preview files without downloading

Play videos and audio, see images, and read PDFs, Word, Excel and text files right in Kipuu, without filling up your computer.

📱

Kipuu on iPhone and Android

Your company's files in your pocket: browse folders, upload photos and documents, and share files from your phone.

🗂️

Trash and version history

Nothing is deleted by accident: deleted items go to the trash, and every upload keeps its earlier versions.

Billing and security

📊

How usage-based pricing works

No plans and no seats to buy upfront. You pay for the storage and the operations your company actually uses.

🧾

Spending cap and invoices

Set a monthly cap so there are never surprises, get warned before you reach it, and download every invoice as a PDF.

🛡️

Two-step verification

Add a 6-digit code from an authenticator app every time you sign in, on the web and in the app.

🔒

How Kipuu keeps your files safe

Encrypted connections, encrypted storage, private links that expire, roles and a full activity log.

👥

Invite your team and choose their role

Invite people by email, choose what each one can do, and remove someone when they leave.

👤

Your account: name, password and devices

Change your name or password and sign out of other devices from My account.

🗑️

Delete your account

How to delete your Kipuu account from the web or the app, and what gets deleted.
