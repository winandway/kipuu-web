How billing works

  1. Start with the free trial. To keep uploading after it, add a card in Billing: we verify it with your bank without charging anything.
  2. With a plan you pay its monthly price, plus extra people and extra storage if you added them. With pay as you go we measure your usage all month. On the 1st we close the month that ended and add it to your balance.
  3. We charge every month, on the 1st. A month with any usage costs at least $1.30 ($1 of service plus $0.30 of card fees), which covers about 34 GB stored all month. A month with no usage costs nothing. You always see the running balance in Usage.
  4. Set your own monthly spending cap. We alert you at 50, 80 and 100 %, and uploads pause at the cap. Downloads keep working.
  5. If a payment fails we email you and try again on days 3, 7 and 14. After that, uploads pause until the card is updated. We never delete your files for a failed payment.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new company gets 10 GB for 14 days, with no card.

When am I charged?

On the 1st of each month, for the month that just ended. A yearly plan is charged once for the whole year. Pay as you go has a $1.30 minimum in a month with usage; a month with no usage costs nothing.

How are people charged?

Plans already include 3, 5, 10 people; each extra person is $2.00 a month. With pay as you go, people are free.

What happens when my plan is full?

Uploads pause until you add 1 TB blocks ($10.00 a month each) or move up a plan. Viewing and downloading keep working.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Change your plan anytime in Usage. A yearly plan can be changed when its year ends. You can move back to pay as you go or delete your account whenever you want.

Where are my files stored?

In data centers in the United States, encrypted at rest with AES-256 and always sent over HTTPS.

What if a payment fails?

We email you and retry on days 3, 7 and 14. After that, uploads pause until you update your card. We never delete your files because of a failed payment.

Which devices does Kipuu work on?

On the web in any browser, Android, Windows and Mac. iPhone is coming soon.
