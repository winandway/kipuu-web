How billing works

  1. Add a card when you create your account. We verify it with your bank without charging anything.
  2. We measure your usage all month. On the 1st we close the month that ended and add it to your balance.
  3. Card fees would eat a charge of a few cents, so small amounts add up: we only charge once your balance reaches $5.00, or once a year, or when you close your account. You always see the running balance in Usage.
  4. Set your own monthly spending cap. We alert you at 50, 80 and 100 %, and uploads pause at the cap. Downloads keep working.
  5. If a payment fails we email you and try again on days 3, 7 and 14. After that, uploads pause until the card is updated. We never delete your files for a failed payment.
