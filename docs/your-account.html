Billing and security

Your account: name, password and devices

Change your name or password and sign out of other devices from My account.

Everything about your own account lives in the My account tab of the panel.

Change your name

Type your name and tap Save. If you close the page before saving, Kipuu keeps what you typed.

Change your password

  1. Type your current password.
  2. Type the new one, with at least 10 characters.
  3. Tap Change password. You stay signed in here, and every other device is signed out.

Sign out of other devices

Lost a phone or used a shared computer? Tap Sign out other devices and Kipuu closes your session everywhere except where you are now.
