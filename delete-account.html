Delete your Kipuu account

  1. Sign in to Kipuu on the web or in the app.
  2. Open the Account menu at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account, type your password and confirm.

Your account, your sessions and, if you are the only person in your company, all its files and folders are deleted right away. Invoices and billing records are kept as the law requires.

Sign in to delete my account

Can't sign in? Write to go@windoce.com from the email of your account and we will delete it for you.
