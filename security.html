Security at Kipuu

How we keep your company's files safe, in plain words.

  • Stored in the United StatesYour files live in data centers in the United States.
  • Encrypted at rest and in transitFiles are stored encrypted with AES-256, and every connection to Kipuu uses HTTPS.
  • Private by defaultStorage is never public. Links to open or upload a file are signed and expire in minutes; links you share expire in 1, 7 or 30 days and can be turned off.
  • Your company, separatedEach company is fully separated from the others. Roles (owner, administrator, editor, viewer) decide what each person can do.
  • Protected sign-inTwo-step verification with an authenticator app, a limit on sign-in attempts, and a shield against bots on the web.
  • Activity logSee who uploaded, shared, moved or deleted what, and when.
  • Nothing lost by mistakeDeleted files stay in the trash for 30 days, and earlier versions can be restored.
  • Payments by StripeCards are handled by Stripe. Kipuu never sees or stores your card number.
